## Optional: share feedback

### No GitHub account? Use the Google feedback form

[Submit the TeacherBox Preview Feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdGBLhPmdePHehzPfEngNtAuO7Q170Zy6uUy1hU9FMrziv-lQ/viewform). You can submit without signing in to Google, GitHub, or TeacherBox; signing in to Google is optional if you want to save your progress.

The form accepts bug reports, usability issues, ideas or feature requests, accessibility feedback, classroom workflow feedback, getting-started or onboarding feedback, and other feedback. Choosing a TeacherBox area, sharing your device/browser, and providing an email address are optional. Email is only for a reply about your report.

Google processes form submissions. Responses are stored with the private form and its linked response sheet for the TeacherBox owner to review. The form is separate from TeacherBox and does not read or attach anything saved in your browser.

**Form privacy reminder:** Do not include student names, school names or identifying school details, grades tied to a student, personal details, screenshots, or other sensitive information.

### Prefer to post publicly on GitHub?

GitHub participation is optional and does not control access to the open TeacherBox preview. If you want to post and do not have an account, [sign up for GitHub](https://github.com/signup), then sign in. After signing in, open the relevant area and choose **New discussion** or **New issue**:

- **General:** [introduce yourself briefly or say what you plan to try](https://github.com/LarryAlexander/teacherbox-preview/discussions/categories/general). You do not need to share your real name, school, or contact information. This is not an application, waitlist, or access request.
- **Ideas:** [share a feature idea](https://github.com/LarryAlexander/teacherbox-preview/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Q&A:** [ask a question](https://github.com/LarryAlexander/teacherbox-preview/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Bugs:** [open the bug report form](https://github.com/LarryAlexander/teacherbox-preview/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).

GitHub issues and discussions are public and associated with the GitHub identity that posts them. Never include student-identifying, school-identifying, or other sensitive or private information. Review each post and any screenshot before submitting.

## Saved work and weather

TeacherBox keeps saved activities and settings in the browser you use. It does not provide account-based or cross-device syncing. Clearing that browser's site data can remove locally saved work.

When you enter a ZIP code for a weather lookup, TeacherBox sends that ZIP code to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for a location search. The selected location is then used to request the forecast. Weather requests do not include your saved lessons or activities.
